My Story

Combining theory with hands-on code

I am Nilansha Pandey, currently pursuing my Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Lucknow. My interest in engineering started during my Diploma in Engineering, where I fell in love with logic and programming. Since then, I have transitioned into deep learning, computer vision, and building high-performance web systems.

With a cumulative CGPA of 8.16, I place a high value on academic excellence, but believe the true test of knowledge is building. Whether I am tuning the weights of a ResNet-18 classifier for BRATS brain tumor MRI datasets or writing custom middleware in Django for database routing, I strive for clean execution and performant architecture.

I regularly participate in hackathons (like the Smart India Hackathon, Unstop, and EY Tech Hackathon) because I enjoy the pressure of building functional prototypes under tight timelines. I am currently seeking research internships where I can contribute to cutting-edge AI developments.
